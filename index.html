About Masa

Hi, I'm Masa 👋

I live in Japan and enjoy helping visitors experience Tokyo in a relaxed and personal way. I'm not here to rush you from one tourist spot to another — I want you to enjoy Japan like you're spending the day with a local friend.

🇯🇵 Japanese & English 🧘 Yoga & Jogging 🍜 Food Lover 🚇 Train Expert 😊 Patient & Friendly 🗺️ First-Timer Friendly
Why "Uncle Masa"?

My niece Anna has called me "Uncle Masa" since she was little. The name became special to me because it feels warm, friendly, and personal. That is also the kind of experience I want to share with visitors — not a rushed tour, but a relaxed day in Japan with someone who feels like a local friend.

Course 1 — Half-Day

A Half-Day
in Asakusa

This is the Asakusa Course itinerary. Every detail can be adjusted based on your pace and interests.

9:00
Hotel pickup
Masa meets you at your hotel. No need to figure out directions — just be ready to enjoy the day.
Morning
Asakusa Walk
Kaminarimon Gate and Senso-ji — the iconic red gate, incense, and fortune slips. Nakamise shopping street for snacks and souvenirs, then a detour into the quiet local back streets.
Around noon
Local Life Experience
A visit to a local supermarket, Daiso (100-yen shop), and Uniqlo — the everyday Japan that most tourists never see.
Lunchtime
Monjayaki Lunch
Monjayaki, okonomiyaki, and yakisoba cooked live on an iron grill in front of you. A lively and delicious local lunch.
Afternoon
Stroll toward the station
After lunch, a slow walk back — browsing souvenirs and taking in the last of the atmosphere.
14:00–15:00
Hotel drop-off
Masa takes you back to your hotel safely with all your bags and souvenirs.
Course 2 — Half-Day

A Half-Day in
Tokyo & Ginza

This is the Tokyo & Ginza Course itinerary. A morning of iconic spots and an elegant lunch.

9:00
Hotel pickup
Masa meets you at your hotel. No need to figure out directions — just be ready to enjoy the day.
Morning
Tokyo Station, Imperial Palace & Ginza
Photo at the red-brick Tokyo Station building. A walk through the Imperial Palace gardens and a stop at Nijubashi. Then on to Ginza — a slow stroll down the main street, a peek at Mitsukoshi's basement food floor, and a photo stop at Kabukiza Theatre.
Lunchtime
Tempura Lunch & Depachika
A tempura counter where the chef prepares each piece fresh in front of you. After lunch, back to the depachika for souvenir shopping.
14:00–15:00
Hotel drop-off
Masa takes you back to your hotel safely with all your bags and souvenirs.
