About Masa

Hi, I'm Masa 👋

I live in Japan and enjoy helping visitors experience Tokyo in a relaxed and personal way. I'm not here to rush you from one tourist spot to another — I want you to enjoy Tokyo like you're spending the day with a local friend.

Whether it's finding the perfect bowl of ramen, figuring out the train system, or discovering a quiet temple tucked behind a busy street — I know this city, and I love sharing it.
