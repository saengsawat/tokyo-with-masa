About Masa

Hi, I'm Masa 👋

I live in Japan and enjoy helping visitors experience Tokyo and nearby areas in a relaxed and personal way. I'm not here to rush you from one tourist spot to another — I want you to enjoy Japan like you're spending the day with a local friend.

Whether it's finding the perfect bowl of ramen, figuring out the train system, or discovering a quiet temple tucked behind a busy street — I love helping visitors experience the small details that make Japan special.

🇯🇵 Japanese & English 🧘 Yoga & Jogging 🍜 Food Lover 🚇 Train Expert 😊 Patient & Friendly 🗺️ First-Timer Friendly
Why "Uncle Masa"?

My niece Anna has called me "Uncle Masa" since she was little. The name became special to me because it feels warm, friendly, and personal. That is also the kind of experience I want to share with visitors — not a rushed tour, but a relaxed day in Japan with someone who feels like a local friend.
