Course 1

Asakusa
5–6 Hour Private Tour

This is our basic Asakusa Course itinerary. We will enjoy this route at a relaxed pace that fits you perfectly.

9:00
Hotel pickup
Masa meets you at your hotel lobby. (Pickup is available for Chuo, Chiyoda, and Taito-ku only.)
Morning
Asakusa Walk
Kaminarimon Gate and Senso-ji - the iconic red gate, incense, and fortune slips. Nakamise shopping street for snacks and souvenirs, then a detour into the quiet local back streets.
Around noon
Local Life Experience
A visit to a local supermarket, Daiso (100-yen shop), and Uniqlo - the everyday Japan that most tourists never see.
Lunchtime
Monjayaki Lunch
Monjayaki, okonomiyaki, and yakisoba cooked live on an iron grill in front of you. A lively and delicious local lunch.
Afternoon
Stroll toward the station
After lunch, a slow walk back - browsing souvenirs and taking in the last of the atmosphere.
14:00–15:00
Hotel drop-off
Masa guides you safely back to your hotel. (Drop-off is available for Chuo, Chiyoda, and Taito-ku only.)
Course 2

Tokyo & Ginza
5–6 Hour Private Tour

This is the Tokyo & Ginza Course itinerary. A morning of iconic spots and an elegant lunch.

9:00
Hotel pickup
Masa meets you at your hotel lobby. (Pickup is available for Chuo, Chiyoda, and Taito-ku only.)
Morning
Tokyo Station, Imperial Palace & Ginza
Photo at the red-brick Tokyo Station building. A walk through the Imperial Palace gardens and a stop at Nijubashi. Then on to Ginza - a slow stroll down the main street, a peek at Mitsukoshi's basement food floor, and a photo stop at Kabukiza Theatre.
Lunchtime
Tempura Lunch & Depachika
A tempura counter where the chef prepares each piece fresh in front of you. After lunch, back to the depachika for souvenir shopping.
14:00–15:00
Hotel drop-off
Masa guides you safely back to your hotel. (Drop-off is available for Chuo, Chiyoda, and Taito-ku only.)
